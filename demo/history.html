Tang Dynasty - Origin & Poetry

Approx. 8th Century

Zhang Ji's poem "Mooring by Maple Bridge at Night" transforms "Feng Bridge" into a cultural icon.

Ming & Qing - Commercial Peak

1368 - 1912

Becomes the national center for rice and bean distribution. The "Feng Hu" becomes the standard measure.

Qing Dynasty - Rebuilding & Resilience

1770 / 1860 / 1867

Rebuilt in 1770, destroyed in 1860, and reconstructed as the current granite arch bridge in 1867.

War Period - Heritage Protection

1937 - 1945

Patriots protect the famous stone stele from looting by creating replicas during the war.

Modern Restoration

Year 1984

Major restoration of the granite structure to preserve its historical appearance for future generations.

Digital Preservation

Year 2020

"Echoes of Maple Bridge" project launched using XR technology to digitally preserve and showcase the bridge's history.

月落乌啼霜满天，
江枫渔火对愁眠。
姑苏城外寒山寺，
夜半钟声到客船。

The moon sets, crows cry, frost fills the sky,
River maples, fishing lights, I lie awake in sorrow.
Outside Gusu City, from Hanshan Temple,
The midnight bell reaches my guest boat.

离心正惘惘，叹息向寒流。
晚翠深枫寺，春光旧画楼。
枫桥灯火夜，江柱水云秋。
谁谓青霄远，回头即旧游。

My heart is lost in parting, sighing towards the cold current,
Evening greenery deepens at Maple Temple, spring light lingers on old painted towers.
Lights of Maple Bridge at night, river pillars amidst autumn water and clouds,
Who says the blue sky is far? Turning back, I see my old journeys.

晚泊依枫岸，依稀似客居。
寒山钟响后，独看古屏书。

Mooring late by the Maple Bank, vaguely like a traveler's stay,
After the bell of Hanshan rings, I alone read ancient screen inscriptions.

七年不到枫桥寺，客枕依然半夜钟。
风月未须轻感慨，巴山此去尚千重。

Seven years since I last visited Maple Bridge Temple,
On my traveler's pillow, still the midnight bell sounds.
No need to sigh lightly at the wind and moon,
From here to Ba Mountains, there are still a thousand layers of peaks.

去年秋晚此江边，曾听寒山半夜钟。
今日重来倍惆怅，满林霜叶对疏松。

Late last autumn by this riverside, I once heard Hanshan's midnight bell,
Coming again today, I am doubly melancholy,
Facing sparse pines amidst a forest full of frosty leaves.

白首重来一梦中，青山依旧对垂虹。
莫嫌野寺寒山晚，犹有疏钟送客风。

Returning with white hair, it feels like a dream,
Green mountains still face the hanging rainbow bridge.
Do not dislike the wild temple's late evening chill,
There are still sparse bells sending off the traveler in the wind.
